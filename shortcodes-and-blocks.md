# Shortcodes and blocks

You can add KN Booking to any page with a block or a shortcode.

| What | Block | Shortcode |
|---|---|---|
| Booking calendar | Booking Calendar | `[kn_booking]` |
| Customer profile | Booking Profile | `[kn_profile]` |

## Booking calendar

Shows the calendar. Customers pick a date and a time, then send a request.

## Customer profile

Lets logged-in customers see their upcoming and past bookings. Depending on your settings they can also edit or cancel a booking. Create a page called "My bookings" and add the Booking Profile block or `[kn_profile]`.

## Add to Google Calendar link

Under **Settings > Integrations** you can show an **Add to Google Calendar** link on the success message after a booking. It opens a pre-filled event in the customer's browser.
